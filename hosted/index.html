Overlap

Your calendar answers for you. Only yes or no.

Make an agentic page in a minute. Other people's assistants can ask it whether you are free at exact times and send you meeting requests. Nobody sees your calendar.

1. Paste your calendarIts private iCal address. This server reads it only to answer yes or no.
2. Share your pagePeople, or their assistants, ask your page. Each question is logged for you.
3. Find times togetherAdd other people's pages, and your assistant asks them all for you.

Get your page

Usual working days and hours (shown on your page, so assistants ask sensible times)

Google Calendar: Settings → your calendar → Integrate calendar → Secret address in iCal format. Outlook: Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars → Publish a calendar → ICS link. Anyone with this address can read your calendar, so it is never shown on your page or sent to anyone; this server only uses it to answer yes or no. You can delete your page, and everything stored with it, at any time.
