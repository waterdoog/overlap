Overlap

Your calendar answers for you. Only yes or no.

Make an agentic page in a minute. Other people's assistants can ask it whether you are free at exact times and send you meeting requests. Nobody sees your calendar.

How should your page know when you are busy?

Get a file: Google Calendar → Settings → Import & export → Export; Outlook → Calendar → Save calendar (or Publish a calendar as ICS, "Can view when I'm busy" for busy times only). Get an address: Google Calendar → your calendar → Integrate calendar → Secret address in iCal format. You can delete your page, and everything stored with it, at any time.

Here: ready in seconds, on this shared site. Your own address: your page gets its own web origin (the strongest isolation), on your own free Vercel account; this page prepares the settings in your browser and nothing is sent to this server.

Deploy your page on your own address

1. Open Deploy to Vercel (it copies the open-source Overlap repository into your account).

2. When it asks for environment variables, paste these two. They were made in this browser; your busy times are inside, with no titles.

PASS_PROFILE

OWNER_KEY (keep it: it signs you in as the owner)

3. Optional, so receipts survive restarts: in the new project, Storage → Create → Blob (private), then redeploy.
