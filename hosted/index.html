Where do I find these?

File: Google Calendar → Settings → Import & export → Export. Outlook → Calendar → Save calendar, or Publish a calendar as ICS with “Can view when I'm busy”.

Address: Google Calendar → your calendar → Integrate calendar → Secret address in iCal format.

2. When it asks for environment variables, paste these two. They were made in this browser; your busy times are inside, with no titles.

PASS_PROFILE

OWNER_KEY (keep it: it signs you in as the owner)
