Share of one target week (80 half-hour slots) a prober learns, by day
setting                                   day1  day2  day3  day7
free/busy sharing                           100%  100%  100%  100%
Overlap, 1 signed page, no weekly cap        23%   23%   45%   90%
Overlap, 10 signed pages, no weekly cap      75%  100%  100%  100%
Overlap as shipped, 1 signed page            23%   23%   45%   45%
Overlap as shipped, 10 signed pages          45%   45%   45%   45%

The weekly cap is a dial: what a 10-page prober learns of one week, by cap (any number of days)
cap   learned
12    15%
24    30%
40    45%
60    75%
80    100%
A cooperative three-person search asks about 6-12 windows per person (eval/simulate.mjs), so a cap of 24-40 leaves room for 2-6 searches a week.
